VPS CONTROL — THIET LAP NOI KY TREN WINDOWS

1. Giai nen ca thu muc ZIP vao may Windows cua anh.
2. Mo START.cmd binh thuong, KHONG chon Run as Administrator.
3. Lan dau se tai cong cu Microsoft/Google (khoang 250 MB).
4. Khi duoc hoi da co khoa stable chua:
   - Chon Yes neu anh da co khoa P12 stable va muon dung lai.
   - Chon No de tao khoa dau tien, giu co dinh tren may nay.
5. Tu nhap mat khau (it nhat 14 ky tu), nhap lai va luu mat khau
   vao trinh quan ly mat khau rieng cua anh. Khong gui qua chat.
6. Chon thu muc backup tren USB/o ngoai hoac thu muc cloud cua anh.
   File backup .p12 da ma hoa; can giu mat khau rieng de phuc hoi.
7. Ky va kiem tra xong se co KET-QUA-GUI-HERMES.zip.
   Chi gui file nay cho Hermes. Khong gui thu muc keys, file backup
   .p12, password.dpapi hoac mat khau.

Khoa chinh: %LOCALAPPDATA%\VPSControlSigner\keys
Phan mem tu dung lai khoa nay o cac lan sau; khong tao khoa moi
neu da co khoa hoac dau vet khoa cu. Neu bi huy giua chung, chay lai.

Bo nay ky dung APK 1.1.0 da kiem thu; khong rebuild APK, khong sua VPS,
khong tu dang release. Hermes nhan APK da ky de verify/publish GitHub.
Sau do anh cai ban stable tren dien thoai va tu ghep noi mot lan.
